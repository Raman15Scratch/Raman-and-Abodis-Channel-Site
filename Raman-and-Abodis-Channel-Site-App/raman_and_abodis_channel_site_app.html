Raman and Abodi's Channel Site just got an app

To make our channel site more accessible, we made an Android version! It's made with median.co, and obviously the app needs Wi-Fi. You can download the APK and source code on the buttons under the text. IMPORTANT: Because the app is probably unsigned Android will show a warning saying to let Google scan the app, since i used median.co with the exact website ui i didn't change the files in it. So press "Show more" then "Install anyway"
