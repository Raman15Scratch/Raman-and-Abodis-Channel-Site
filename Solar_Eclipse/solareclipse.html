My experience of the August 12 2026 Solar Eclipse! (August 13 2026)

Yesterday in August 12 2026, i was excited for the Solar Eclipse, the only problem was THAT THE SOLAR GLASSES WERE SOLD, so i made a pinhole projector. When it started in 19:08, i looked in the pinhole and got these images:

The reason they were low quality was because my eyes could see better than my phone's camera, and second, there were clouds, so you couldn't see the eclipse good after 20:15, but the city started to get dark and then because it was soon winter time, it became dark naturally.
